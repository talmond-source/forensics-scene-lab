Securing and documenting the scene

Case 26-0417 · 14 Ashcroft Lane, home study · Johnston PD

At 07:52 a resident called to report finding a household member unresponsive on the floor of the home study. The caller stated the decedent had been working late and appeared to have fallen and struck their head on the corner of the desk. Patrol confirmed death at 08:04. The medical examiner's investigator has completed a preliminary body examination and left a note at the outline.

You are the first evidence technician on scene. You will not collect anything today. Collection is a later shift, by someone who was never here and who will rely entirely on the record you leave behind. Your whole job is to make that record honest and complete.

1. Secure. Decide who leaves, where the perimeter goes, how you get in, and who gets written down.
2. Walk through. Look. Touch nothing, mark nothing, photograph nothing. Find every feature in the room.
3. Overall photographs. Four corners, before a single marker goes down.
4. Mark and photograph each item. Marker first, then a midrange that shows where it sits, then a close-up with scale.

Stay on your approach path. The floor between the doorway and the body is where transfer evidence is most likely, and every step you take off the path is a step you will be asked about later.

Controls: W A S D walk · drag to look, or P for mouse-lock · E examine or capture · M marker mode · L oblique light · Shift move faster. Some things will not show up under normal room light.
